## B. German potential: autumn 2019 forecast against today

- Potential growth 2014-19: 1.58 % a year in the 2019 vintage, 1.42 % today.
- 2019 vintage: potential to grow 1.42 % a year in 2019-21; extended at that rate, potential in 2025 would be 8.8 % above 2019. Today's estimate: 3.4 %. Write-down against the Commission's own 2019 projection: -5.1 log points.
- Output gap 2018: +1.20 % then, +1.44 % today; 2019: +0.20 % then, +1.32 % today (end-of-sample estimates are revised; here the 2019 gap was revised up by more than a point).

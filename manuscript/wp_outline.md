# Working paper: outline (draft 25.11.2026)

Working title: **Adjusting at the hiring gate: Germany's 2022-25 slump, labour-market buffers and the missing reform signal**

Target: working paper, about 8,000 words plus appendix; English; Harvard citations; tables and figures; code in the repository.

Core claim: In 2022-25 Germany lost output on the scale of earlier recessions, but unemployment rose little because the adjustment ran through fewer hirings rather than more dismissals, cushioned by demography, the narrow reach of the shock and labour-market institutions. The burden fell on entrants and the unemployed. Because German reform of core labour-market institutions has historically followed rising unemployment, not falling output, this kind of adjustment sends no reform signal: political pressure turns to location, energy and bureaucracy instead.
